About The Yatra

The Spirit of YUVATMA Yalagur

YUVATMA brings youth and families together for spiritual grounding, joy, and collective service at one of Karnataka's most revered Hanumantha Kshetras.

🛕

Sri Kshetra Yalagur

Located in Nidagundi taluk, Sri Yalagureshwara is famous for blessing seekers with fearlessness, mental peace, and renewed vitality.

🎶

Soulful Bhajan Sandhya

An immersive musical evening with traditional instruments, stotras, and devotional singing that touches the core of your soul.

🤝

Uplifting Seva & Sangha

Connect with thoughtful, motivated youth. Learn teamwork, simplicity, and humility through meaningful temple seva.

🍲

Pure Satvik Prasada

Wholesome, freshly prepared traditional Karnataka satvik food served with pure love and devotion across all two days.

🎒 What to Bring & Guidelines

  • Attire: Traditional, comfortable, and modest Indian clothing suitable for temple premises.
  • Essentials: Reusable water bottle, personal toiletries, and any personal daily medications.
  • Weather Gear: A light shawl or jacket for cool early mornings and evening breeze.
  • Identification: A valid government photo ID card (Aadhaar / Driving License).
  • Spirit: An open heart, cooperative mindset, and enthusiasm for group seva and bhajans!
